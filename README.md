## 📖 Overview

A free, registration-free, multilingual web application designed to help international students and foreign workers prepare for Japan's **Specified Skilled Worker (特定技能1号 - Tokutei Ginou Type 1)** certification exams.

To eliminate the steep learning curve caused by complex industrial Kanji and the lack of native-language study materials, this platform provides:
1. **Real exam format practice questions** with full Japanese **Furigana (Ruby)** support.
2. **Instant explanations in 5 languages** so learners can understand concepts deeply in their native tongue.
3. **Interactive 3D Flashcards** for vocabulary memorization.
4. **Category Weakness Diagnostic Reports** to visualize study progress.
5. **Full PWA & Offline Support** for uninterrupted study anywhere.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏢 1. Coverage Across 6 Major Industry Sectors
* 🌾 **Agriculture (農業):** Cultivation basics, crop management, harvest/storage, livestock, safety & hygiene.
* 🏗️ **Construction (建設):** Civil engineering, architecture, infrastructure & lifelines, site rules & culture.
* 🚗 **Automobile Maintenance (自動車整備):** Engines, chassis, tools & measurement, vehicle inspection & adjustment.
* 🏭 **Food Manufacturing (飲食料品製造業):** Food hygiene, manufacturing processes, HACCP standards, occupational safety.
* 🍽️ **Food Service (外食業):** Cooking methods, hygiene management, customer service & hospitality.
* 🏨 **Lodging (宿泊業):** Front desk operations, customer communication, planning/PR, Japanese hospitality.

---

### 🌍 2. Five Supported Languages
All UI controls, explanations, and vocabulary definitions are fully translated and verified across:
* 🇯🇵 **Japanese (日本語)**
* 🇺🇿 **Uzbek (O'zbek)**
* 🇬🇧 **English**
* 🇳🇵 **Nepali (नेपाली)**
* 🇲🇲 **Burmese (မြန်မာဘာသာ)**

---

### 📝 3. 1,740 Practice & Mock Exam Questions
* **290 questions per sector** (Core foundations + practical applied questions + mock exam simulation).
* **Instant grading** with detailed explanations.
* **"Finish Test Anytime" button:** Answer 5, 12, or any number of questions and immediately view your results.
* **Dual-Language Review:** View mistakes and explanations in both Japanese and your native language side-by-side.

---

### 🗂 4. 6,000+ Technical Vocabulary & 3D Flashcards
* **1,000+ essential industry words per sector** with phonetic Hiragana furigana and localized meanings.
* **Interactive 3D Flip Card:** Tap or press Space/Enter to flip between the term and its translation/notes.
* **Smart Memory Feedback:** Mark words with *"Still learning"* (saved directly to your Mistake Notebook) or *"Got it!"*.
* **Multi-script Search:** Instant filtering by Kanji, Hiragana, Katakana, Romaji, or translation keywords.

---

### 📊 5. Category Weakness Diagnostic Reports
* At the conclusion of any quiz or mock exam, an analytical report breaks down your performance across each subcategory.
* Visual progress bars and status badges (⭐️ *Good*, ⚠️ *Needs Review*, ❌ *Weak spot*).
* Direct one-click navigation to review relevant vocabulary flashcards.

---

### 📓 6. Mistake Notebook (間違いノート)
* Automatically saves incorrect test answers and unmemorized vocabulary words.
* Allows targeted review without re-reading the entire question bank.

---

### 📲 7. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Capability
* Fully installable on iOS, Android, and Desktop as a standalone application.
* Cached via Service Worker so learners can study on trains, flights, or in areas with poor internet connection.

---

## 🛠 Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3, Modern Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+). Zero heavy frameworks for maximum load speed (< 1 second).
* **Backend & Hosting:** Google Firebase Hosting (Global CDN), Cloud Firestore, Firebase Anonymous Authentication.
* **Offline Engine:** Web App Manifest & Service Worker Cache API.
* **Quality Assurance:** Node.js automated test suites verifying 100% data integrity across all 1,740 questions, 6,000+ vocabulary entries, and SEO schemas.
