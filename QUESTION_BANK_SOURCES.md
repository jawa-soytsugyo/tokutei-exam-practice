# Question-bank scope and official sources

Last checked: 2026-08-16

The public Japanese exam bodies do **not** publish a bank of 1,000 current or past
questions for any of these six fields. They publish exam scopes, study materials,
and a limited number of examples. The questions in this project are therefore
new, unofficial practice items aligned to those public scopes. They must not be
described as official, leaked, or reproduced past-exam questions.

## Coverage implemented here

| Field | Authored base questions | Generated practice variants | Additional items | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Agriculture | 90 | 1,080 | 0 | 1,080 |
| Automobile maintenance | 90 | 1,080 | 0 | 1,080 |
| Construction | 90 | 1,080 | 1,000 vocabulary drills | 2,080 |
| Food manufacturing | 90 | 1,080 | 0 | 1,080 |
| Food service | 90 | 1,080 | 0 | 1,080 |
| Lodging | 90 | 1,080 | 0 | 1,080 |

Each mock bank uses 90 independently authored situations. Twelve neutral exam
phrasings and rotating sets selected from at least five plausible distractors
produce 1,080 deterministic practice entries. They are a broad drill bank, not a
claim that 1,080 confidential official questions were obtained.

## Agriculture / 農業

Official content owner: National Chamber of Agriculture / 全国農業会議所.

- [Official scope and standards](https://asat-nca.jp/asat1/overview/cover)
- [Official textbooks and examples](https://asat-nca.jp/asat1/textbook)
- [Official implementation rules](https://asat-nca.jp/jp/images/procedure_for_the_asat_jp.pdf)
- [Current Prometric delivery information](https://www.prometric-jp.com/ssw/test_list/archives/5.)

The bank covers general crop farming, livestock farming, occupational safety,
facilities and machinery, cultivation and husbandry, pests and disease,
harvesting and shipment, hygiene, and practical recognition/judgment.

## Automobile maintenance / 自動車整備

Official content owner: Japan Automobile Service Promotion Association / 日整連.

- [JASPA exam scope, notices, and examples](https://www.jaspa.or.jp/mechanic/specific-skill/index.html)
- [Current Prometric delivery information](https://www.prometric-jp.com/ssw/test_list/archives/7skn)
- [Official first-level exam summary](https://www.jaspa.or.jp/Portals/0/resources/jaspahp/user/specific-skill/pdf/1_2024_001.pdf)

The bank covers engine and chassis fundamentals, inspection, diagnosis, repair,
adjustment, fuels and lubricants, tools and measuring devices, disassembly and
assembly, batteries and charging, steering, suspension, brakes, tires, and safe
work. Official theory and practical-judgment sections use different formats; the
site's large bank is intended for repeated single-choice practice.

## Construction / 建設

Official content owner: Japan Association for Construction Human Resources / JAC,
under Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism rules.

- [JAC exam overview and examples](https://jac-skill.or.jp/exam/?lang=ja)
- [JAC multilingual study-material portal](https://global.jac-skill.or.jp/examination/documents.php)
- [MLIT implementation rules](https://www.mlit.go.jp/tochi_fudousan_kensetsugyo/content/001519991.pdf)

The mock bank covers common site knowledge, safety, law and site culture, civil
engineering, building construction, and infrastructure/equipment theory and
practical judgment. It supplements the existing 1,000 construction vocabulary
drills; vocabulary drills are not counted as 1,000 independent exam scenarios.
The repository does not currently retain complete publisher/license metadata for
the legacy glossary source, so that provenance must be confirmed before the
glossary is redistributed beyond uses permitted by its source.

## Food and beverage manufacturing / 飲食料品製造業

Official content owners: Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and
OTAFF / 外国人食品産業技能評価機構.

- [MAFF sector page](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/sanki/soumu/tokuteiginou.html)
- [Current implementation guideline](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/sanki/soumu/attach/pdf/tokuteiginou-114.pdf)
- [OTAFF exam information](https://otaff.or.jp/tokutei/insyoku-1/)
- [OTAFF official study materials](https://otaff.or.jp/tokutei/insyoku-1/materials/)

Coverage is weighted toward food safety, general hygiene, process control, and
HACCP, with occupational safety also represented. Judgment, planning, and
calculation items are included.

## Food service / 外食業

Official content owners: Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries and
OTAFF, with study materials from the Japan Foodservice Association.

- [MAFF current sector page](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/gaisyoku/gaikokujinzai.html)
- [Current implementation guideline](https://www.maff.go.jp/j/shokusan/gaisyoku/attach/pdf/gaikokujinzai-113.pdf)
- [OTAFF exam information](https://otaff.or.jp/tokutei/gaisyoku-1/)
- [OTAFF official study materials](https://otaff.or.jp/tokutei/gaisyoku-1/materials/)
- [Japan Foodservice Association study hub](https://www.jfnet.or.jp/project/%E5%A4%96%E5%9B%BD%E4%BA%BA%E6%9D%90%E3%81%AE%E6%88%A6%E5%8A%9B%E5%8C%96%E3%81%B8%E5%90%91%E3%81%91%E3%81%9F%E5%8F%96%E3%82%8A%E7%B5%84%E3%81%BF/)

The 90 base questions are divided equally among hygiene management, food and
drink preparation, and customer service, following the official equal question
counts across those three areas. Official score weights are not used as item
counts here. Judgment, planning, and calculation scenarios are included.

## Lodging / 宿泊

Official content owner: Center for Accommodation Industry Proficiency Test / CAIPT,
under Japan Tourism Agency rules.

- [CAIPT specified-skills exam page](https://caipt.or.jp/tokuteiginou)
- [Current Prometric delivery information](https://www.prometric-jp.com/ssw/test_list/archives/12hotel)
- [Japan Tourism Agency implementation guideline](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/001733699.pdf)
- [National Ryokan Hotel Association study resources](https://www.zenryoren.jp/useful/)

The bank balances front desk operations, planning and public relations, customer
service, restaurant service, and safety/hygiene/basic knowledge, with practical
scenarios concentrated in guest-facing work.

## Copyright and review policy

Official text, examples, photographs, drawings, logos, and translations remain
the property of their publishers. Public download availability does not grant a
right to republish them. This project links to those materials for scope and
provenance but uses newly written prompts, answers, distractors, and explanations.
Any future imported question must keep stable source metadata and pass the local
bank validator before publication.
